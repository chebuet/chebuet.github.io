Research Interests

Biotechnology & CRISPR-Dx Biosensing & Point-of-Care Testing Microfluidics Infectious Disease Diagnostics Global Health Technologies Environmental Pollution Nucleic Acid Amplification-Free Detection Aptamer-Based Biosensors

Dr. Mohammad's research focuses on developing next-generation CRISPR-based diagnostic tools for low-resource settings. Working at the interface of chemical engineering, molecular biology, and global health, he has pioneered novel CRISPR-Cas12a detection mechanisms and reporter systems that enable ultrasensitive, amplification-free detection of infectious pathogens including HIV. His doctoral work in Dr. Qingshan Wei's lab at NC State produced landmark publications in Angewandte Chemie, Nucleic Acids Research, and Communication Chemistry, and he was recognized with numerous prestigious recognitions, including 2024 CoE Scholar of the Year (Research) title, Vivian T. Stannett Fellow, Tau Beta Pi Distinguished Student Lectureship (honorable mention), Ferrell Outstanding PhD Graduate Award (honorable mention), etc. for research excellence.
